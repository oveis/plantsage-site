# Plantsage Privacy Policy / 개인정보 처리방침

_Last updated: September 15, 2026_

Plantsage ("the app") is operated by an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. 한국어 안내는 아래에 있습니다.

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Garden data (beds, plants, tasks, logs, harvest records) | Sync and backup of your garden | Our server (AWS, hosted in the US) |
| Approximate location (only while using the app) | Detect your growing zone and show local weather | Converted to a zone/coordinates; coordinates are sent to our weather provider |
| Photos you submit for diagnosis, identification, or seed scanning | AI analysis to answer your request | Sent to our server and processed by Anthropic's Claude API; diagnosis/identification photos are not stored after processing. Journal photos you attach are stored with your journal |
| Chat messages to the AI gardener | Generate AI responses | Processed by Anthropic's Claude API via our server |
| Device identifier (random UUID) and, if you sign in with Apple, your Apple user ID and email | Account and sync; recognizing your subscription | Our server |
| Subscription status | Unlock Premium features | Verified with Apple, stored on our server |

We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

## Service providers

We share data only with the processors needed to run the app: Apple (sign-in, subscriptions), Amazon Web Services (hosting), Anthropic (AI processing), and OpenWeatherMap (weather, receives coordinates only).

## Retention & deletion

Your data is kept while you use the app. You can delete all app data at any time from **Settings → Reset All Data**, which also deletes your data from our server. For any remaining questions or deletion requests, contact us at the address below.

## Children

Plantsage is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and revise the date above.

## 수집하는 정보

- **정원 데이터** (화단·식물·할 일·일지·수확 기록): 동기화와 백업을 위해 서버(미국 소재 AWS)에 저장됩니다.
- **대략적 위치** (앱 사용 중에만): 재배 지역(Zone) 판별과 날씨 표시에 사용됩니다. 좌표는 날씨 제공사에 전달됩니다.
- **진단·식별·스캔용 사진**: AI 분석을 위해 서버를 거쳐 Anthropic Claude API로 처리되며, 처리 후 저장하지 않습니다. 일지에 첨부한 사진은 일지와 함께 저장됩니다.
- **AI 정원사 대화 내용**: 응답 생성을 위해 Claude API로 처리됩니다.
- **기기 식별자(무작위 UUID)** 및 Apple 로그인 시 Apple 사용자 ID·이메일: 계정·동기화·구독 확인에 사용됩니다.
- **구독 상태**: 프리미엄 기능 잠금 해제에 사용됩니다.

광고 식별자 수집, 분석/트래킹 SDK, 데이터 판매는 **하지 않습니다**.
