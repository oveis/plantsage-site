We usually reply within a few days. Please include your app version (Settings → Version) and a description of the issue.

## FAQ

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open the App Store app → your profile → Subscriptions → Plantsage → Cancel. Or in Plantsage: Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Reset All Data removes everything from your device and our server.
